About Me

My name is Al Francis, also known online as laet4x.

I am a cybersecurity professional working in red team operations and AI agentic development. I have more than 10 years of experience across software development, systems architecture and cybersecurity operations.

I created OSIF, an open-source OSINT framework, and co-founded Project Access Granted Society (Project-AG), PEHCON and KALASAG. I run free security seminars and CTF workshops for the Philippine cybersecurity community.
